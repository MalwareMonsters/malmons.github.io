About Malmons World

Malmons, also known as Malware Monsters, was created by Lena Yu aka LambdaMamba to bring malware to life as (un)natural creatures.

We aim to advance the world’s cybersecurity through creativity, art, storytelling, and education via the Malmons universe. By reimagining real malware threats as collectible creatures, Malmons helps demystify cybersecurity and make malware analysis more accessible to a wide range of ages, cultures, and backgrounds.

Our mission is to blend technical education with playful, approachable design — using visual art and narrative to make cybersecurity understandable and exciting for all.

What We Offer

At Malmons World LTD, we provide a variety of services to help integrate Malmons into your own projects:

  • 🎨 IP licensing of existing Malmons characters
  • 🖼️ Custom artwork of existing Malmons for your use — including vector art, 3D models, and animations
  • 🧬 Custom Malmons designs based on your favorite real-life malware samples
  • 🤝 Creative collaboration with companies, events, educators, and organizations

We’re excited to build partnerships that bring cybersecurity to life in new and imaginative ways.
