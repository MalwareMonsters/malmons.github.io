🎲 Malware & Monsters

Malware & Monsters is an innovative, tabletop-inspired cybersecurity training game. Designed in the spirit of Dungeons & Dragons, this game transforms traditional incident response learning into an immersive, collaborative storytelling experience.

Teams work together to analyze, respond to, and overcome digital threats — each represented by unique creatures called Malmons (short for Malware Monsters).

These Malmons are based on real-world malware behavior and characteristics. Each has distinct abilities, weaknesses, and traits, mirroring the actual threats security professionals face in the wild. The creature-collection mechanic paired with team-based decision making makes this game not only educational but also highly engaging and memorable.

Whether you’re a security team looking for hands-on training, an educator introducing malware analysis in the classroom, or a cyber nerd looking for your next creative campaign — Malware & Monsters is designed to meet you there.
